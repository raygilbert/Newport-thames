Privacy Policy Support

Get help

Email hello@newportthames.com. We read every message and aim to reply within a few business days. Please do not include your child's name or other personal details.

For community help and bug reports, visit Wren Discussions on GitHub. The source code is also on GitHub.

Wren is built and maintained by one person in spare time, so replies are not instant.

Common questions

Does Wren cost anything?

No. It is free, with no ads or in-app purchases.

Does it need the internet?

No. Wren's core features work offline. The internet is used if a caregiver chooses to fetch pictures while importing a board.

Nothing is speaking when I tap a tile. What should I check?

Check that the device volume is up and the silent switch is off. Then confirm that the device has a text-to-speech voice installed. On iPhone and iPad, look under Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content. On Android, look under Settings → System → Languages → Text-to-speech output. Restart Wren after installing a voice.

Can I change the voice or its speed?

Voice and speed come from the device's speech settings.

How do I edit or add tiles?

Open the caregiver area, then edit the board. Caregiver controls are kept apart from the child's view so a child does not change a board by accident.

Can I move my boards to another device?

Yes. Export a board file from the caregiver area and import it on the other device. Wren uses the open Open Board Format, so the files also work with other communication apps.

Where is my data stored?

Wren stores boards and settings locally on your device. Device backups may also include this data. We have no copy and cannot restore it for you. Export your boards from time to time if they matter to you.

Can you add my language or a symbol?

Tell us by email or in Wren Discussions. Wren is open source, so requests and contributions are welcome.
