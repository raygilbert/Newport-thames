Privacy Policy

How Wren handles the boards, settings, images, and speech used by families.

Privacy Policy Support

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Who we are

Wren is a free communication app for young children and the adults who support them. It is published by Newport-Thames, LLC (“we”). You can reach us at hello@newportthames.com.

The short version

Wren does not collect, transmit to us, sell, or share personal information. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Boards and settings are stored locally on your device. Your device may include that local app data in device-managed cloud backups. A caregiver can also choose to fetch online images while importing a board; the device's speech engine may use its own services. The details are below.

What stays on your device

The boards, tiles, and phrases you create or edit, the settings a caregiver chooses, and any boards you import are stored in your device's local storage. We never receive them. We cannot see, recover, or delete them for you because they are not on our servers. Removing the app removes its local data; device backups may still contain a copy.

When the app uses the internet

Wren's core features work offline. When a caregiver imports a board file that links to pictures hosted online, the app asks permission before requesting them. The caregiver can fetch the images or skip them. If you choose to fetch them, your device connects directly to the server named in the file. That server can see your device's network address, as with any web request. We do not operate that server or see the request. If you skip the images, Wren does not request them.

Speech

When a child taps a tile, Wren sends the words to the speech engine built into the phone or tablet. Wren does not record audio or use the microphone. Depending on the device and selected voice, the operating system's speech engine may process speech through its own services, including network voices. That processing is governed by the device maker's policy, not ours.

Device backups

Your device may include Wren's stored data in its own backups, such as iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad or Google backup on Android. Those backups belong to you and the platform provider. We do not have access to them.

Children

Wren is designed for young children, used with a caregiver. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing about a child for us to collect, store, or disclose. The app contains no advertising, in-app purchases, social features, or external links a child can follow without a caregiver.

Artwork and open source

Wren is open source software licensed under Apache 2.0. Its symbols are ARASAAC pictograms by Sergio Palao for the Government of Aragón, used under a Creative Commons BY-NC-SA licence. The Wren character artwork is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. Full credits appear in the app's caregiver area and project repository.

Permissions

On iOS, Wren requests no permissions. On Android, Wren requests only internet access, used for the consented image import described above. It does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the effective date. Any change that would let the app collect personal information would be a major change to the app and would be described in the release notes.
